    How to play

    • Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of your yard onto your start square.
    • Move pawns clockwise around the board, then up your coloured home lane. You need the exact roll to reach home.
    • Land on an opponent to capture it and send it back to its yard.
    • Squares with a star and every start square are safe: no captures there.
    • Rolling a 6, capturing, or getting a pawn home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
    • First to bring all four pawns home wins.

    Tap the ROLL button or the die to roll, then tap a glowing token. Drag to rotate the board, pinch or scroll to zoom.

    Install on iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.
