    How to play

    • Everyone starts on the START pad. Roll the die and move that many squares, 1 → 100.
    • Land at the foot of a ladder 🪜 and you climb straight up it.
    • Land on a snake's head 🐍 and it swallows you: you slide down to its tail.
    • Rolling a 6 gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
    • You need the exact roll to land on 100 (or bounce back, if you turned that on in Settings).
    • First to reach 100 wins!

    Boards: Easy has lots of ladders, Hard is full of long snakes, and Random builds a new board each time.

    Tap the die on your badge, the 3D die or the board to roll (Space on a keyboard). Drag to rotate, pinch or scroll to zoom.
